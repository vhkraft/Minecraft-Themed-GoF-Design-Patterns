--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RECOMMENDED LEARNING ORDER FOR CREATIONAL DESIGN PATTERNS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

LEVEL 1: THE FOUNDATIONS OF OBJECT CREATION
These patterns introduce the fundamental concepts of controlling single-instance access 
and decoupling client code from concrete constructors.

1. Singleton (Ender Dragon Boss Instance Tracker)
- Why first: It is the most direct conceptual entry point. It teaches you how to control 
     instantiation, ensure thread safety, and provide a single global point of access.

2. Factory Method (MobSpawner Interface)
- Why second: Builds on basic instantiation by introducing polymorphism, allowing subclasses 
     or factory methods to decide which concrete classes to instantiate dynamically.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LEVEL 2: SCALING UP TO FAMILIES & COMPLEX OBJECTS
Once you grasp single-class creation, these patterns handle cohesive product families 
and multi-step configurations.

3. Abstract Factory (Biome Asset Factory)
- Why third: Extends factory logic to manage multiple, related product families simultaneously 
     ensuring they work together seamlessly without tight coupling.

4. Builder (Enchanted Trident Builder)
- Why fourth: Solves the telescoping constructor problem by separating complex, step-by-step 
     object configuration from its final representation, complete with strict validation rules.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LEVEL 3: RUNTIME REPLICATION & STATE DUPLICATION
The most advanced creational pattern focused on duplicating existing instances.

5. Prototype (Combat Fox Clone)
- Why last: Moves away from standard construction logic entirely, teaching you how to 
     instantiate complex objects efficiently at runtime by cloning pre-existing master prototypes.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
